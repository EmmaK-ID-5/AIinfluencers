What it is

A network of colleagues across OLIVER and BTG+ who use AI in their day-to-day roles and share their experience on Viva Engage Storylines. Not AI experts. Not tool reviewers. Real colleagues showing real workflows, prompts, lessons learned and use cases from the work they already do. 💬

You choose which Influencers to follow based on what is relevant to your role, team or region. Their posts then land in your Viva Engage feed, so practical AI examples find you, instead of the other way round. 📥
